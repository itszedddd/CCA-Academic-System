[88] ABSTRACT
[89] 	As Calvary Christian Academy experiences growth in its student population and administrative operations, the existing fragmented and manual system for managing student records, enrollment, tuition payments, and report generation has become inefficient, resulting in data redundancy, delays in processing, increased workload, and a higher risk of errors. This study developed an AI-Enhanced School Management System to streamline and improve the school’s data management processes.
[90] The system features a centralized, web-based platform that securely stores and manages student information, enabling faster access and improved accuracy. It incorporates AI Auto-Fill Enrollment to reduce repetitive data entry, AI Error Detection to identify inconsistencies, and an AI Report Generator to produce academic and financial reports instantly. A digital tuition tracking module was also implemented to enhance payment monitoring and record accuracy.
[91] The implementation of the system improved operational efficiency, reduced administrative workload, minimized data redundancy, and enhanced the accessibility and reliability of records. It also enabled faster report generation and better monitoring of student and financial data, supporting more effective decision-making and overall school management.
[95] CHAPTER 1
 INTRODUCTION
[97] Artificial Intelligence (AI) has provided new opportunities to enhance information systems by assisting users in completing tasks more efficiently. AI-powered assistance can support information retrieval, provide intelligent responses, reduce repetitive work, and improve decision-making while allowing users to maintain control over administrative processes.
[98] The continuous advancement of technology has influenced the education sector by improving how institutions manage academic, administrative, and financial processes. School management systems have become essential tools for organizing student information, streamlining workflows, and improving access to accurate data. However, many educational institutions still encounter challenges caused by manual processes, disconnected platforms, and increasing administrative responsibilities.
[99] This study introduces CCA EduSys: AI-Enhanced School Management System with AI Assistance for Calvary Christian Academy, a centralized platform designed to improve the management of student records, enrollment, tuition monitoring, academic information, and administrative processes. Through AI assistance integrated into different system modules, the proposed system aims to improve efficiency, accuracy, accessibility, and user experience among school administrators, staff, students, and parents.
[100] Background of the Study
[101] Calvary Christian Academy (CCA), located at Blk 1 Lot 9 & 10, Sarmiento Townville Subdivision, Barangay Poblacion I, City of San Jose del Monte, Bulacan 3023, is a private educational institution offering basic education from Kindergarten to Grade 10. The school manages various operations involving student enrollment, academic records, tuition payments, and communication with parents. Its workforce consists of approximately 20 teachers and administrative personnel, including the Principal, Registrar Staff, Cashier, IT Staff, Librarian, Guidance Counselor, and School Nurse, who handle different academic and administrative responsibilities.
[102] Currently, CCA manages student information using multiple platforms and manual procedures, resulting in repeated data entry, fragmented records, and difficulties in accessing information efficiently. During enrollment, student information is collected through several forms, causing the same details to be encoded approximately six (6) to ten (10) times per student. This process increases administrative workload, creates data redundancy, and increases the possibility of inconsistencies in student records. The enrollment and tuition monitoring processes also experience efficiency issues due to manual handling. Enrollment requires manual form submission, document checking, and encoding, resulting in an average processing time of 11–20 minutes per student and waiting times of approximately 21–30 minutes during peak periods. Meanwhile, tuition payments are monitored using spreadsheets and physical receipts, which increases verification workload and contributes to payment record errors caused by manual tracking and updating. The management and retrieval of academic and administrative information require significant time and effort. Administrative staff spend approximately 10–15 hours per week managing and updating paper-based student records, while school-wide report preparation requires approximately 10–12 hours. The use of multiple storage locations makes information retrieval less efficient and may delay reporting, decision-making, and communication with stakeholders.
[103] To address these challenges, this study proposes CCA EduSys: AI-Enhanced School Management System with AI Assistance for Calvary Christian Academy. The proposed system will provide a centralized platform that integrates student records, enrollment management, tuition monitoring, academic information, and reporting processes. AI assistance will be incorporated across different modules, including Registrar, Admission, Cashier, Student/Parent Portal, Principal Dashboard, and IT Administration, to support users through intelligent information retrieval, system guidance, and faster access to relevant data. The system aims to reduce redundant tasks, improve operational efficiency, and provide a more accessible and user-centered school management solution.

Furthermore, research indicates that technology must demonstrate a strong "task-technology fit," meaning the system's capabilities must align precisely with the specific academic requirements of the user to improve satisfaction and performance (Alyoussef, 2021).

[204] Synthesis
[205] The reviewed school management systems, including VaultEdu PH, Classe365, EdFleet, Skoolia, Smart School, EduManage, Edusuite, and Smart Eskwela, demonstrate how digital platforms improve educational administration through centralized data management, automation, and improved accessibility of school information. These systems provide essential features such as student information management, enrollment processing, attendance monitoring, financial management, reporting, and communication tools. Furthermore, the integration of artificial intelligence features such as virtual assistants, automated reporting, predictive analytics, and intelligent data processing shows the potential of AI in reducing administrative workload and supporting faster decision-making within educational institutions.
[206] However, most existing systems are designed as general solutions and may require further customization to meet the specific operational needs of individual schools. While some platforms provide AI-powered features, these are often limited to specific functions rather than providing continuous AI assistance across multiple user roles and school processes. To address these limitations, this study proposes CCA EduSys: AI-Enhanced School Management System with AI Assistance for Calvary Christian Academy, which focuses on integrating centralized school management with AI assistance across different modules, including Registrar, Admission, Cashier, Student/Parent Portal, Principal Dashboard, and IT Administration. The proposed system aims to improve information accessibility, reduce repetitive tasks, support users in completing their responsibilities, and enhance the overall efficiency of school operations.
[208] Statement of the Problem
[209] Calvary Christian Academy manages student enrollment, academic records, and tuition payments using multiple platforms, resulting in fragmented information management, repeated data entry, and delays in accessing and processing important records. As the number of students and administrative responsibilities continues to increase, the school faces challenges in maintaining accurate, organized, and accessible information. This creates a need for an improved system that enhances efficiency, reduces redundant work, and streamlines processes such as enrollment, tuition monitoring, and report generation to support faster decision-making, improved communication, and smoother school operations.
[210] General Problem
[211] Calvary Christian Academy experiences difficulties in efficiently managing student records, enrollment, tuition payments, and report generation, resulting in increased administrative workload, redundant data entry, and delays in accessing, processing, and managing information.
[212] Specific Problems
[213] Student information is recorded multiple times across different forms and files (approximately 6–10 repetitions per student), requiring administrative staff to spend approximately 10–15 hours per week managing paper-based student records. This results in data redundancy, increased workload, and a higher risk of inconsistencies in student information. 
[214] Accessing student records and tuition information requires approximately 10–15 minutes per request due to records being stored across multiple systems, resulting in reduced efficiency in information retrieval. 
[215]  The preparation of school-wide academic and financial reports requires approximately 10–12 hours, leading to delays in releasing information and supporting administrative decision-making. 
[216] Tuition payments are currently tracked using spreadsheets and physical receipts, increasing administrative workload and the likelihood of verification errors, with an estimated five (5) payment-record errors occurring per month. 
[217] The enrollment process relies on manual form submission and document handling, requiring approximately 11–20 minutes of processing time per student and resulting in waiting times of approximately 21–30 minutes per student during peak enrollment periods.
[219] Objectives of the Study
[220] General Objective
[221] To develop an AI-enhanced School Management System that improves the efficiency, accuracy, and accessibility of student information management while reducing redundant data entry, administrative workload, and delays in enrollment, tuition tracking, and report generation at Calvary Christian Academy.
[222] Specific Objectives
[223] To reduce data redundancy and administrative workload by implementing a centralized student information database that stores student records once, minimizes repeated data entry, and reduces paper-based record management time from 10–15 hours per week to 2–5 hours per week while improving the accuracy and accessibility of student information across all system modules. 
[224] To improve the accessibility of student records and tuition information by implementing a centralized system that reduces retrieval time from 10–15 minutes to 1–5 minutes per request and integrating an AI-powered assistant to help users quickly search for records, answer record-related inquiries, and facilitate faster access to information. 
[225] To automate the generation of academic and financial reports, reducing report preparation time from 10–12 hours to 5–10 minutes per report, and integrate an AI-powered assistant that assists users in retrieving, summarizing, and interpreting report information for faster and more informed decision-making. 
[226] To enhance the accuracy and efficiency of tuition payment monitoring by implementing a digital payment tracking system integrated with centralized student records, reducing payment-record errors from five (5) per month to 0–1 per month, and integrating an AI-powered assistant to help users retrieve payment information, respond to payment-related inquiries, and support faster verification of tuition records. 
[227] To enhance the enrollment process by reducing manual work through automated data entry and document handling, decreasing processing time from 11–20 minutes to 5–8 minutes per student and reducing waiting time from 21–30 minutes to 10–15 minutes per student.
[228] Theoretical Framework
[230] Figure 9. Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) &
[231] Task-Technology Fit (TTF) Theory.
[232] This study is anchored on the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) and Task-Technology Fit (TTF) Theory. These theories explain how the proposed CCA EduSys: AI-Enhanced School Management System with AI Assistance for Calvary Christian Academy improves school operations, addresses administrative inefficiencies, and supports user adoption.
[233] The Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) by Fred D. Davis emphasizes Perceived Ease of Use and Perceived Usefulness as factors influencing technology adoption. In this study, Perceived Ease of Use refers to how easily administrators, registrars, teachers, admission staff, and cashiers can use the system for enrollment, student records, tuition monitoring, and report generation. Perceived Usefulness refers to how the system improves work performance by reducing repetitive data entry, minimizing encoding errors, speeding up record retrieval, and automating academic and financial reports. These functions address the identified problems of fragmented records, redundant encoding, manual tuition tracking, and time-consuming report preparation.
[234] The Task-Technology Fit (TTF) Theory by Goodhue and Thompson states that technology improves performance when its features match user tasks. School personnel perform tasks such as enrollment processing, student record management, tuition monitoring, payment verification, and report preparation. The proposed system incorporates AI Auto-Fill Enrollment, AI Error Detection, AI Report Generation, and centralized cloud-based record management to support these tasks. This alignment helps reduce data redundancy, shorten processing and retrieval time, improve record accuracy, and streamline school operations.
[235] By integrating TAM and TTF Theory, this framework explains how users are likely to adopt the proposed system and how its features support the actual administrative tasks of Calvary Christian Academy. These theories provide the foundation for developing a user-friendly, efficient, accurate, and task-oriented school management system that improves enrollment management, student record management, tuition monitoring, and report generation for faster and more reliable decision-making.

[246] Scope and Delimitation of the Study
[247] Scope of the study
[248] This study focuses on the design, development, and implementation of CCA EduSys: An AI-Enhanced School Management System with AI Assistance for Calvary Christian Academy. The system provides a centralized, web-based platform for managing student information, enrollment, academic records, tuition payment records, report generation, and administrative processes.
[249] The proposed system includes six major modules: the Registrar Module, Admission Module, Cashier Module, Student/Parent Portal, Principal Dashboard, and IT Administration Module. These modules are designed to streamline school operations while providing role-based access to authorized users.
[250] The Teacher Module enables teachers to access their assigned class lists, manage student grades, monitor academic records, and submit academic information within the system. It also provides AI assistance for information retrieval and answering system-related inquiries to help teachers perform their administrative tasks more efficiently.
[251] The Registrar Module enables authorized personnel to manage student records, academic information, and record retrieval through a centralized database. It also provides AI-assisted search to improve the accessibility and retrieval of student information.
[252] The Admission Module supports online enrollment, student information collection, document management, and AI-assisted enrollment guidance. These features are intended to simplify enrollment procedures and reduce processing time.
[253] The Cashier Module manages tuition payment records, payment monitoring, receipt management, and AI-assisted payment inquiries. This module improves payment tracking and facilitates faster verification of student payment information.
[254] The Student/Parent Portal allows students and parents to access student information, academic records, and tuition payment status. It also provides AI assistance to answer common inquiries and improve access to school-related information.
[255] The Principal Dashboard provides access to academic, enrollment, and financial reports for monitoring school operations. AI-assisted report summarization supports administrators in reviewing information more efficiently for decision-making.
[256] The IT Administration Module is responsible for user account management, system maintenance, security, and access control. Only authorized IT personnel can create, modify, suspend, reactivate, reset passwords, and assign user roles for system users.
[257] Artificial Intelligence is integrated into the system as an assistive technology that supports intelligent search, natural language inquiries, information retrieval, process guidance, report summarization, and user assistance. The AI features are intended to improve user productivity and operational efficiency without replacing human decision-making.
[258] The system will be implemented and evaluated within Calvary Christian Academy to assess its functionality, usability, reliability, and effectiveness. The evaluation will be guided by the Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) and Task-Technology Fit (TTF) framework to measure user acceptance and task support.
[259] Delimitation of the Study
[260] The study is limited to the development of a web-based school management system for Calvary Christian Academy. It is designed specifically to support the school's administrative operations and organizational workflow.
[261] The system is limited to managing student information, enrollment records, academic records, tuition payment records, report generation, and user account administration. It does not replace the responsibilities or professional judgment of teachers, administrators, or other school personnel.
[262] The AI features are limited to intelligent search, natural language inquiry support, information retrieval assistance, enrollment guidance, report summarization, and user assistance. The system does not implement predictive analytics, machine learning forecasting, automated grading, behavioral analysis, recommendation systems, or autonomous decision-making.
[263] The system does not integrate with external payment gateways, learning management systems, government education databases, or other third-party platforms. Payment monitoring is intended solely for recording and managing school payment information within the system.
[264] The system is custom-built for the operational requirements of Calvary Christian Academy and does not support multi-campus deployment or implementation in other educational institutions without further customization.
[265] The proposed system is a web-based application that requires a stable internet connection for access and operation. Offline functionality and dedicated mobile applications are beyond the scope of this study.

[393] CHAPTER 2
 METHODOLOGY
[394] This section focuses on analyzing the existing administrative and record-keeping processes of Calvary Christian Academy and identifying the root causes of inefficiency in enrollment, academic record management, tuition tracking, and report generation. Based on the results of interviews, observations conducted among the principal, registrar, teachers, and administrative staff, several recurring issues were identified. These include redundant data entry across multiple forms (6–10 repetitions per student), slow access to student records and tuition information due to fragmented storage, time-consuming report generation (10–12 hours), manual tuition tracking using spreadsheets and receipts, and delays in enrollment processing. These findings revealed the urgent need for a centralized, AI-enhanced school management system capable of automating data entry, error detection, and report generation, thereby improving efficiency, accuracy, and accessibility. Thus, the proposed AI-Enhanced School Management System was conceptualized to address these specific issues and modernize the overall administrative operations of the school.
[396] System Analysis
[397] Requirements Documentation
[398] The requirements documentation outlines the essential features and system constraints necessary to address the problems observed and feedback gathered from staff. The proposed AI-Enhanced School Management System includes both functional and non-functional requirements.
[399] Functionally, the system must allow staff to enter student information once, which is automatically synced across all modules through the AI Auto-Fill Enrollment feature, reducing redundant data entry. It should enable recording, updating, and monitoring of academic performance and tuition payments in a centralized database. The AI Report Generator will produce instant academic and financial reports, eliminating the long hours currently spent on manual report preparation. An AI Error Detection feature will identify inconsistencies or missing information in student records, ensuring accuracy and integrity. Staff must be able to quickly search and retrieve student records, grade histories, and tuition information to support faster decision-making. Role-based access will ensure that only authorized personnel can edit records while others have view-only permissions.
[400] Non-functionally, the system must be user-friendly, reliable during peak periods such as enrollment or tuition collection, and secure, with login authentication and role-based access to safeguard sensitive information. The system should be scalable to accommodate a growing number of students and administrative tasks, and it must perform all updates, AI auto-fills, error detection, and report generation in real-time to streamline operations.
[401] Current System Utilized
[402] Currently, Calvary Christian Academy relies on a combination of manual and semi-digital processes. Student information is stored physically in filing cabinets and digitally through spreadsheets and Google Drive, while tuition payments are tracked using Excel and physical receipts. Teachers can view student grades through Google Drive, but only administrative staff can update records. The IT staff serves as the system administrator.
[403] Although this approach ensures that student information is eventually recorded, it suffers from redundancy, inefficiency, and a lack of real-time access. Administrative staff spend 10 to 15 hours per week managing records, and preparing academic or financial reports can take 10 to 12 hours. These delays hinder timely decision-making and communication with parents. The absence of a centralized system limits accessibility, scalability, and overall operational efficiency, particularly as the number of students and administrative tasks increases.
[404] Therefore, there is a clear need for a centralized AI-Enhanced School Management System to automate repetitive tasks, improve record accuracy, streamline report generation, and enhance overall administrative operations.
[405] The New System (System Design)
[406] Requirement Analysis
[407] Based on interviews with school administrators and staff, observations of current administrative workflows, and document reviews, several critical issues were identified in the existing student information and record management system at Calvary Christian Academy. These include repetitive data entry across multiple forms, lengthy report preparation times, fragmented storage of student and tuition records, and limited real-time access to essential information. These challenges contribute to increased administrative workload, inefficiency, and delays in decision-making.
[408] To address these issues, the proposed AI-Enhanced School Management System was designed with both functional and non-functional requirements in mind.
[409] The functional requirements include automated enrollment through Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to reduce redundant manual data entry, centralized storage and management of student information, academic records, and tuition payments, AI-powered error detection to flag inconsistencies, an intelligent report generator to produce instant academic and financial reports, and role-based access control to secure sensitive data. The system also supports real-time monitoring of student academic progress and tuition status.
[410] The non-functional requirements focus on system reliability and availability during peak enrollment and payment periods, user-friendly interface design to accommodate staff with varied technical skills, data security to protect student and financial information, scalability to support future growth in student population, and cloud-based accessibility for remote use and centralized data management.
[411] To fulfill these requirements, the system employs modern technologies including Python with FastAPI for back-end development, SQLite/PostgreSQL databases for secure and efficient data storage, and web technologies styled with TailwindCSS for a responsive user interface. AI functionalities are integrated through libraries such as PyTesseract for OCR and machine learning modules for academic and financial data analysis.
[412] Research Design and Methodology
[413] This study utilized a descriptive-developmental research design combined with mixed-method data collection. The descriptive phase involved analyzing current record management practices at Calvary Christian Academy to identify inefficiencies and bottlenecks. The developmental phase focused on designing and building the AI-Enhanced School Management System to address the identified problems.
[414] Qualitative data were gathered through structured interviews with school staff and direct observation of administrative processes such as enrollment, grading, and tuition tracking. Quantitative data were collected via questionnaires from administrative personnel measuring factors such as time spent on data entry, report generation duration, and accessibility of student information.
[415] Respondents included 20 teachers and 1 Principal, 2 Registrar Staff, 1 Cashier, 1 IT Staff, 1 Librarian, 1 Guidance Councilor, and 1 School Nurse, encompassing those directly involved in student records and tuition management. Convenience sampling was applied to ensure a representative sample within the school workforce.
[416] Agile methodology was adopted to guide system development. The process involved iterative cycles of prototyping, user testing, and feedback incorporation, ensuring that the final system effectively meets the operational needs and usability expectations of the school.
[417] Instrument Used in the Study
[418] Data collection instruments included structured interviews conducted with administrative and teaching staff to understand current challenges and desired system features. Field observations during peak enrollment and report preparation periods documented workflow inefficiencies and bottlenecks. Secondary research on similar school management systems helped identify best practices and effective technological solutions. Additionally, questionnaires with quantitative measures were distributed to gather empirical data on current system performance and user satisfaction.
[419] Statistical Treatment of Data
[420] Collected data were analyzed using descriptive statistics. Frequency counts and percentage distributions summarized responses regarding task durations, system usage frequency, and report preparation times. Mean values were calculated to determine average time spent on administrative tasks and typical delays in report generation. These analyses provided a clear understanding of current system limitations and guided the system design process to address key inefficiencies.
[421] Ethical Consideration
[422] The study adhered strictly to ethical standards. Participation by staff was voluntary, with clear explanations of the study's purpose and assurances of confidentiality and anonymity. Respondents were free to withdraw at any time without consequence and were encouraged to ask questions for clarity. All data collected were securely stored and used exclusively for research purposes, ensuring respect and protection for all participants throughout the study.

This structure directly addresses repeated data entry and supports the AI Auto-Fill Enrollment feature. The Enrollment_Form entity digitizes the enrollment process by storing forms, school year data, and documents, reducing manual paperwork, while the Academic_Record tracks student performance per subject and quarter for efficient grade monitoring and automated report generation. The Attendance entity records student presence along with the responsible user, improving accountability and real-time monitoring, while the Risk_Assessment entity introduces an AI-driven component that stores predicted risk levels, reasons, and dates to enable early intervention for at-risk students.
[477] To maintain data accuracy, the Error_Log entity records inconsistencies such as duplicate or missing data, supporting the AI Error Detection feature. Financial management is handled through the Tuition_Payment, Payment_Record, and Receipt entities, where tuition details, transaction logs, and proof of payment are stored, improving accuracy, traceability, and efficiency in tuition tracking while reducing reliance on physical receipts. The Report entity enables the generation of academic and financial summaries linked to system users, supporting the AI Report Generator feature and significantly reducing report preparation time. Overall, the ERD presents a centralized and well-structured database system that integrates all major school operations, minimizes redundancy, enhances data accuracy, reduces administrative workload, and improves decision-making through AI-supported features such as auto-fill enrollment, error detection, risk assessment, and automated reporting.
